# The Care Loop — Six Terms, Six Distinct Jobs

Source: [`assets/careloop-six-terms.png`](../../assets/careloop-six-terms.png) (Business Plan 2026, slide 11)

Core features. Six terms, six distinct jobs.

| Term | Job |
| --- | --- |
| **Trust Score** | The headline result of the product evaluation: health, planet, longevity, ethics. |
| **Trust Index** | The detailed view behind the Trust Score. Closer to a credit score and credit report than two separate measures. |
| **Care Print** | Total of care impact by avoiding harm to planet and society. |
| **Ownership Log** | Everything the customer puts into the application during usage. The customer's own contributed record. |
| **Care Pass** | The certification of ownership and care. |
| **Care Hub** | A community led tips and conversations around product care. |
