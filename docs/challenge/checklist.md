# CareLoop — Success Criteria Checklist

Source: [`01-careloop-challenge.md`](01-careloop-challenge.md) "Qualities of a successful case", plus AI capability framing from [`02-careloop-brief.md`](02-careloop-brief.md).

Scope: step 04 "Use fully" of the ownership loop. Ownership Log, warranties, care info, maintenance and repair history.

---

## 1. A data structure, not a mockup

- [ ] A defined way to structure and classify product and care data so it could later slot into a real EU Digital Product Passport.
- [ ] Stated goal is a data structure that could feed a real passport, not a throwaway prototype.

## 2. AI carrying weight

- [ ] AI doing genuine work through scanning, scoring, or an agentic assistant, rather than tacked on at the end.
- [ ] Capability list to draw from: scan, interpret, predict, recommend, assist, learn.
- [ ] Learn entry asks to use feedback to improve future recommendations, build trust, make uncertainty visible.

## 3. Visible cause and effect

- [ ] Makes the pull of everyday actions on a product's lifespan visible enough that owners actually want to look after what they own.
- [ ] Behaviour chain: Notice -> Understand -> Decide -> Act.
- [ ] The goal is not simply to remind people. The goal is to help them make better decisions on what happens to a product next.

## 4. Honest, independent take

- [ ] An honest, independent take on Care Loop's vision from a team that gels.
- [ ] Named in the challenge as the real differentiator.
