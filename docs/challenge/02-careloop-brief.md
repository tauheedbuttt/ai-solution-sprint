# The Care Loop — AI Sprint Challenge Brief

Source: [`assets/careloop-brief.pdf`](../../assets/careloop-brief.pdf)

An intelligent ownership companion that helps people understand the products they own, care for them, extend their life, and dispose of them responsibly. All in one place.

**OWN BETTER. USE FULLY. WASTE LESS.** — www.thecareloop.app

## 01 Context: ownership is fragmented after purchase

People:

- forget maintenance
- miss early signs of problems
- postpone repairs
- replace products too early
- struggle to find the right information or service
- struggle to prove resale value
- hesitate on the best next life for their products

Main goal: support informative, timely, and easy actions to help products stay in use for longer.

## 02 Mission

Make product ownership and care easy, convenient, and economically attractive by increasing access to information, providing actionable data for decision-making, simplifying access to services, and helping shift behaviors around ownership and waste.

## 03 The solution: full ownership loop in five steps

| # | Step | What happens |
|---|------|--------------|
| 01 | Scan or ask | Identify the product. |
| 02 | Get the score | Evidence-led Trust Score. Ethics, Planet, Health, Longevity. |
| 03 | Decide better | Compare with other products. |
| 04 | **Use fully** (challenge focus) | Warranties, care information, maintenance and repair history in one place. Ownership Log. |
| 05 | Next life | Repair, resell or recycle. Product history. Care Pass. |

Every action writes to the Ownership Log. The Care Pass certifies the product history.

## 04 Meet the product owner

Busy, practical. Wants to save money, time, and waste less. Wants more value from what they own.

They want to:

- Avoid unnecessary replacements and save money
- Repair products when possible
- Resell or pass on products at a better value
- Know what to do at different stages of a product's life
- Have more agency over their environmental impact

They struggle with:

- Finding reliable information about care, repair, resale, and end-of-life options
- Knowing when a product should be maintained, repaired, replaced, resold, or recycled. Product care falls down the priority list until something goes wrong.
- Understanding which action is the best option
- Finding the time to research and make decisions
- Knowing what to do when something goes wrong

## 05 Customer goals

BUY → CARE → REPAIR → RESELL → NEXT LIFE

A person owns a household appliance for several years.

- What would make them care at the right moment?
- What could trigger an interaction?
- What information or support would help them decide what to do?
- How could you make the next action easy and worthwhile?
- What happens if they ignore or postpone it?
- How could the experience make the value of caring, repairing, keeping, or passing on the product visible?

The goal is not simply to remind people. The goal is to help them make better decisions on what happens to a product next.

**Notice → Understand → Decide → Act**

## 07 Why product lifecycle data matters

EU regulation and sustainability reporting are pushing companies to understand what happens to products throughout their lives.

| Regulation | What it means | Why it matters for this challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Product Passport (DPP) | A digital record of product information and lifecycle data, including relevant repair and traceability information. | Data about care, repairs, condition and product history can become part of the product's ongoing record. |
| Right to Repair | Strengthens consumers' ability to repair products and requires longer availability of certain repair support and spare parts. | Companies need better visibility into what breaks, what gets repaired and what support owners need. |
| EPR / ESPR | Policies increasingly place responsibility on producers for product sustainability, circularity and lifecycle impacts. | Companies need to understand what happens to products after purchase and at end of life. |
| CSRD / Scope 3 | Companies report on environmental impacts across their value chain, including relevant downstream impacts. | Real-world information about use, lifespan, repair, resale and disposal can help build a clearer picture of product impacts. |

## The sprint challenge

> How can we engage product owners to take action to extend the life of the household appliances they own?

## 08 Potential AI applications

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| Scan | Identify an appliance, model, component, error code or visible condition. |
| Interpret | Translate technical product or usage data into plain-language meaning. |
| Predict | Identify likely maintenance needs, durability, abnormal patterns or moments of intervention. |
| Recommend | Choose the next best action based on product, owner context and available services. |
| Assist | Answer questions and potentially take actions across the ownership log. |
| Learn | Use feedback to improve future recommendations. Build trust, make uncertainty visible. |
