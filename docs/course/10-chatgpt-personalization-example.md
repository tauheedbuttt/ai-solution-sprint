## Copilot example prompt and response

Copilot highlights:
1. Challenger Sales Model
   - Teach: reframe way customers view their business and needs
   - Tailor: link capabilities to customer's individual goals to overcome barriers and align with customer needs/job functions
   - Take Control: pursue goals directly but non-aggressively
2. Attributes of a Challenger
   - Challengers proactive in challenging customer's thinking
   - Provide unique insights customer hasn't considered before
3. Reframing
   - Ability to reframe or share insights in new way is crucial
   - Reframing helps differentiate salesperson and move towards challenger approach
4. Insight Selling
   - Emphasises importance of providing valuable insights rather than just solutions
   - Based on concept from Harvard Business Review article "The End of Solution Sales" (2012)
5. Quotes and Perspectives
   - Includes quote from Seth Godin about rewarding those who break rules and innovate
