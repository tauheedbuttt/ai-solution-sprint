# General Info on Using AI Tools

- Major challenge of language models and AI: they can produce content that sounds highly professional but may include factual errors or contradictions
- Do not use language models to produce final content. Instead, use them for formatting existing text, analysing or generating ideas for content you will write yourself. Language models useful for editing and polishing text, e.g. grammar and clarity. Do not use AI as source
- Image generation more complicated. Copilot and OpenAI (ChatGPT) allow image use (e.g. DALL-E), but user must ensure no copyrighted elements are included
- Confidential information, such as trade secrets, should not be entered into AI tools. Many free AI tools store input and may reuse it for training or other users
