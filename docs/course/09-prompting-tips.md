# ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini - Prompting Tips

- Find ready-made prompts online, search blogs etc. (e.g. "Best prompts about xxx")
- Good method: ask AI directly, e.g. "I'm sales and marketing professional. How can you help me with xxx, considering xxx..."
- Also check: snackprompt.com
- E.g. if starting company or new service, use AI to brainstorm and develop ideas step by step. Or drafting solution/proposal for client, use AI to help structure it. OR now in event while working on challenges
- For improving text: "Please be critic, analyse this text and let me know how it could be improved."
- Thoughts from AI experts: "In few years, knowledge work will revolve around prompting, those best at it will succeed."
